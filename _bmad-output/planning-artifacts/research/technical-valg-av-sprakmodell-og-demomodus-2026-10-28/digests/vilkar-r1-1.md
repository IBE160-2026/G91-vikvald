## Findings (claims)

1. **Claim:** Mistral free mode: "we may use your data (input and output) to train our artificial intelligence models", with an opt-out right. For pay-as-you-go: "Customers retain full control over this processing and have the right to opt out at any time." Thumbs up/down feedback always authorizes training on that input/output. The page covers Mistral Studio/Vibe and does not name "La Plateforme" explicitly.
   Source: https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347617-do-you-use-my-user-data-to-train-your-artificial-intelligence-models · Mistral AI · pub_date: "updated over a week ago" (~Oct 2026) · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium (wording suggests paid is opt-out too, not opt-in) · class: data-terms
2. **Claim:** Mistral DPA, effective 27 July 2026, applies to commercial customers/Controllers, not consumers. Mistral uses customer data as controller for training "unless Customer is or has opted-out of training". Transfers to restricted countries are allowed with safeguards. Data becomes inaccessible 30 days after termination.
   Source: https://legal.mistral.ai/terms/data-processing-addendum · Mistral AI · 2026-07-27 · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium (read via summarizer) · class: regulatory/data-terms
3. **Claim:** Mistral: "By default, your data is hosted in the European Union", unless you use the US API endpoint. Some features may temporarily transfer data outside the EU to the sub-processors listed in the Trust Center.
   Source: https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347629-where-do-you-store-my-data-or-my-organization-s-data · Mistral AI · date not shown · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium (from search snippet of the official page) · class: data-terms
4. **Claim:** Mistral offers "Custom structured outputs" (your own schema) and JSON mode. Custom structured outputs are recommended.
   Source: https://docs.mistral.ai/capabilities/structured-output/custom_structured_output/ · Mistral AI · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium · class: feature
5. **Claim:** Azure Foundry/Azure OpenAI: prompts and completions are "NOT used to train any generative AI foundation models without your permission or instruction" and are not available to OpenAI. Standard deployments process data in the customer geography. An EU DataZone deployment processes in "that or any other European Union Member Nation". Global deployments (and Batch) may process in any geography. Data at rest and the abuse-monitoring store stay in the customer geography. EEA deployments have EEA-based human reviewers. Data processing is governed by the Microsoft Products and Services DPA.
   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy · Microsoft · ms.date 2026-05-18 (updated 2026-06-05, just over 3 months old) · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: data-terms/regulatory
6. **Claim:** Azure OpenAI supports structured outputs with `response_format: {type: "json_schema", json_schema: {strict: true, ...}}`. A Q&A thread reports json_schema not supported on one model (gpt-chat-latest-2026-05-05 via Responses API), so check per model.
   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/how-to/structured-outputs · Microsoft · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium (search summary) · class: feature
7. **Claim:** Azure for Students: school email, no credit card, $100 credit, renews yearly. Marketing copy mentions access to Azure OpenAI.
   Source: https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students · Microsoft · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium (search summary. Model and quota availability on student subscriptions not verified) · class: pricing
8. **Claim:** Gemini API terms: Unpaid Services data is used to improve Google products, with human review. Paid Services data is not used for that, only logged for a limited time for abuse detection. "If you're in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK, the terms under 'How Google uses Your Data' in 'Paid Services' apply to all Services." Also "You may use only Paid Services when making API Clients available to users in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK." Paid Services fall under the Data Processing Addendum.
   Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms · Google · last updated 2026-04-28 (about 6 months old) · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: data-terms/regulatory
9. **Claim:** Vertex AI: "Google won't use your data to train or fine-tune any AI/ML models without your prior permission or instruction". In-memory and durable caching are on by default (deleted within 24 h). Cached data stays in the selected location for regional/multi-regional endpoints. The global endpoint may serve from any region.
   Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/data-governance · Google Cloud · last updated 2026-10-28 · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: data-terms
10. **Claim:** Anthropic first-party API: `inference_geo` is only "global" (default) or "us". Workspace geo (storage at rest) is only "us". On Bedrock and Google-managed Vertex, region is determined by the endpoint or inference profile.
    Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency · Anthropic · n/d (current) · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: data-terms
11. **Claim:** Bedrock: model providers have no access to Bedrock logs or customer prompts/completions, because models run in AWS-owned deployment accounts.
    Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-protection.html · AWS · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: data-terms
12. **Claim:** Bedrock structured outputs: JSON schema via `output_config.format` for Claude (InvokeModel) or `outputConfig.textFormat` (Converse), plus `strict: true` tools. Supported with cross-region inference. Some geographic profiles don't support it for every model (example: Haiku 4.5 via the `in.` profile). Limits: at most 12 optional properties, `additionalProperties: false` required, first-schema compilation up to ~90 s.
    Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/structured-output.html · AWS · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: feature
13. **Claim:** Bedrock geographic cross-region inference profiles (eu.*) keep requests within Regions of that geography. Some newer models in eu-west-1 must be called via an inference profile.
    Source: https://docs.aws.eu/bedrock/latest/userguide/cross-region-inference-support.html (seen only as a search result) plus secondary mer.vin (2026-06) and repost.aws · AWS / third party · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium · class: data-terms
14. **Claim:** Sikt KI is a national platform for chat and assistants with Feide login. Data "is not used to train language models, and never leaves the EU". Organizations need an agreement with Sikt. A "build AI into your website" embed exists, but no public API keys or developer API were found.
    Source: https://ki.sikt.no/en · Sikt · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: medium (absence of evidence) · class: feature/data-terms
15. **Claim:** Ollama supports JSON schema structured outputs: "Provide a JSON schema to the `format` field."
    Source: https://docs.ollama.com/capabilities/structured-outputs · Ollama · n/d · accessed 2026-10-28 · confidence: high · class: feature
